About

A builder at the intersection of Digital Finance and Africa

I'm Heritage Falodun, a fintech founder, Bitcoin protocol researcher, and product developer based in Africa. I've spent the last three years building at the frontier of digital money on the continent.

I believe Bitcoin is the most important monetary technology of our era, and that its greatest impact is yet to come in emerging markets. Africa has the demographics, the need, and the infrastructure for digital assests, AI and Bitcoin-native financial system.

My work spans founding companies, contributing research to SSRN and Bitcoin Magazine, consulting on digital asset growth strategy, and advocating for sustainable Bitcoin mining using Nigeria's abundant flare gas.
