About Heritage

Heritage leads the intersection of digital currency research, risk-aware payments design, and African financial transformation. His work spans policy, product strategy, and market-first Bitcoin infrastructure.

At 22, Heritage co-founded DigiOats, then launched Neona Synergy and Spendin Africa by 25. He currently supports enterprise and community programs with Bitcoin-first payments, compliant stablecoin rails, and sustainable mining strategies.

His mission is to make Bitcoin a practical tool for economic empowerment, bridging global protocol innovation with Africa’s growth markets.
