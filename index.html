About Heritage

His work spans protocol research, payments innovation, and African financial transformation.

Heritage co-founded DigiOats, Neona Synergy, and Spendin Africa, and serves as Bitcoin Consultant and Growth Lead at DnarHQ. He is a Bitcoin Correspondent and Moderator for the Black Bitcoin Billionaires community, and a contributing writer for Bitcoin Magazine.
