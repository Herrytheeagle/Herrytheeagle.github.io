About

Builder at the intersection of Digital Finance, Agent Payments and Artificial Intelligence

I'm Heritage Falodun, a fintech founder, Bitcoin protocol researcher, and product developer. I've spent the last five years building at the frontier of digital money in Africa and the global south.

I believe digital payment infrastructure with agentic capabilities using stablecoins and Bitcoin is the most important monetary technology of our era, and that its greatest impact is yet to come in emerging markets. Africa has the demographics, the need, and the infrastructure for digital assets, AI and Bitcoin-native financial systems.

My work spans founding companies, contributing research to SSRN and Bitcoin Magazine, consulting on digital asset growth strategy, and advocating for sustainable Bitcoin mining using Nigeria's abundant flare gas.
